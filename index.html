About me

Graphic designer and AI-powered visual specialist with 16+ years in branding, digital marketing, print, social media, video editing and corporate identity.

I have worked in-house and in agencies across the UAE and Pakistan, from printing houses to management consultancies, partnering with marketing, sales and leadership teams. My job is to take an idea, give it a clear visual form, and make it work on every screen and surface it lands on: a reel, a billboard, a LinkedIn post or a product listing.

Today I combine a classic Adobe workflow with AI image and video tools, which lets me move from concept to finished campaign faster without losing craft.
